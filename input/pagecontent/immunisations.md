### Status
{: .underlined}

This domain is currently under development.

### Clinical description
{: .underlined}

This domain is expected to contain records of immunisations relevant to a patient's immunisation history.

### Intended use
{: .underlined}

Support visibility of a patient's immunisation history.

### Source information
{: .underlined}

An appropriate national immunisation information source is expected to provide this information.

### Privacy and confidentiality considerations
{: .underlined}

Normal SDHR privacy, confidentiality, security, access, and audit controls apply to immunisation information.

### Clinical safety considerations
{: .underlined}

Production interpretation should follow the authoritative source, coverage, status, and data-quality guidance when that guidance becomes available.

### Known limitations
{: .underlined}

Implementation guidance, including source coverage, historical depth, update behaviour, and data-quality guidance, is still being developed.
